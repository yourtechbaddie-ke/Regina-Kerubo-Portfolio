WHAT WORKED

The hero product gave every channel one commercial story. Creator proof reduced hesitation, while inventory and deadline messaging created urgency without a discount-led strategy.

WHAT CHANGES NEXT

Move the strongest creator cut earlier in the funnel, test a shorter waitlist nurture sequence and use the post-purchase UGC opt-in to seed the next launch before production begins.
