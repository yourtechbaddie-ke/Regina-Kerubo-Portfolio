CLIENT BRIEF

L'Oréal Paris.
Make beauty personal.

PORTFOLIO SPEC
NOT COMMISSIONED

L'Oréal Paris already operates at the intersection of accessible beauty, self-expression, science and cultural visibility. This spec campaign imagines a creator-led launch system that turns that brand promise into something audiences can see people actually doing: getting ready, testing, comparing, layering, travelling, going out, taking the product into ordinary life and making it their own.

The creative platform is Worth the Frame. Every creator receives the same strategic truth — beauty is not a performance for approval; it is a choice made for yourself — but each creator interprets that truth through a different routine, skin tone, aesthetic and audience relationship. The result is not six copies of one advertisement. It is one campaign world with multiple believable lives inside it.

OBJECTIVETurn product consideration into creator-led proof.
AUDIENCE18–40 beauty shoppers, social-first, routine-led.
PRIMARY CHANNELTikTok + Instagram Reels + paid social.
SUCCESSQualified product interest, saves, shares and conversion.
