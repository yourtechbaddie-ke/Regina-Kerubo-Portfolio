01 / THE STRATEGY

Make the next step
feel inevitable.

From Scroll to Sale is a social-commerce campaign framework built around one simple principle: every piece of content should earn the next action.

Instead of pushing a cold audience straight to checkout, the campaign creates a warm path through curiosity, proof, conversation and personalised follow-up.
