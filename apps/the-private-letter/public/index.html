Campaign readout — ready.

TPL-Q3-001 / FINAL
WHAT WORKED

Specific aspiration beat generic luxury language.

Variant B led on opens and clicks because it made the desired outcome concrete. The campaign should keep its restrained visual language while making the promise more specific.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

Retarget the engaged 4,812.

Build the next send around destination affinity and recent browsing behaviour. Suppress the unengaged segment and protect deliverability before scaling volume.

COMMERCIAL SIGNAL

386 contacts reached opportunity stage.

Move qualified responders into the CRM sales queue, preserve campaign attribution and use consultation completion as the next optimisation event.
