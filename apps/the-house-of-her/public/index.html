DEAR READER,

I’m not particularly interested in being put in one little box.

There. We may as well start with that.

I’m Regina — a woman building a life that refuses to stay in one lane. I’m a software engineering student and aspiring engineer, a cybersecurity learner, a digital creator and marketer, a luxury travel entrepreneur in the making, a gamer and game-development enthusiast, a novelist and poetess, and a self-taught chef who genuinely believes a good meal can tell a story before anyone says a word.

That sounds like a lot because, frankly, it is. But it doesn’t feel like a collection of random hobbies to me. It feels like a constellation. Each thing teaches me how to see the others differently.

Code teaches me structure. Writing teaches me emotion. Marketing teaches me attention. Cybersecurity teaches me caution. Travel teaches me curiosity. Food teaches me patience. Games teach me worlds. Motherhood teaches me that plans are adorable suggestions.

I am interested in the space where imagination becomes something people can actually touch, use, remember or feel.

So, welcome to the slightly chaotic, carefully curated, occasionally coffee-fuelled little universe behind the portfolio.

ROOM I · THE WOMAN

A WORK IN PROGRESS — WITH GOOD LIP GLOSS

Soft enough to feel.
Stubborn enough to build.

There is a version of ambition that looks very polished from the outside. The neat desk. The perfect morning routine. The five-year plan in a tasteful little notebook.

My reality is more interesting.

I am learning how to become the woman I imagine while simultaneously being the woman I already am. Some days I am deep in a technical problem. Some days I am thinking about a campaign concept. Some days I am researching a place I want to send travellers. Some days I am writing a scene. Some days dinner needs to happen immediately.

And sometimes I am all of those women before lunch.

That is one of the biggest things I have learned about identity: you don't always discover yourself by choosing one thing. Sometimes you discover yourself by noticing the thread connecting everything.

“I don’t want a smaller life just because it is easier to explain.”

ROOM III · THE LIFE
motherhood, unfiltered

Then there is
my little person.

✿

Becoming a first-time mom has added an entirely different rhythm to my life. Motherhood has this funny way of taking every confident declaration you have ever made — “I’ll just finish this first,” “I’ll sleep early tonight,” “I have a plan” — and gently placing it on the floor.

It has challenged me in ways ambition alone never could.

There is the obvious juggling: learning, building, creating, working, thinking ahead, and still being present for a child who does not care that a deadline is approaching. There are days when my attention is split into tiny pieces. There are days when I am tired and still need to show up. There are moments when I wonder whether I am moving quickly enough, doing enough, becoming enough.

And then there are the tiny moments that reset the whole equation. A laugh. A surprise. A little hand. A completely ordinary day that suddenly feels enormous because someone is growing up right in front of me.

Motherhood has made ambition more complicated — but also more meaningful. I am no longer only imagining the woman I want to become. I am thinking about the example I want my child to see.

I want my child to grow up knowing that a woman can be loving and ambitious, nurturing and curious, soft and formidable.

So yes, sometimes the work gets interrupted. Sometimes the schedule falls apart. Sometimes I have to start again tomorrow. But perhaps that is not failure. Perhaps it is simply a different definition of progress.

Motherhood has not made me less ambitious.

It has given my ambition a heartbeat.
